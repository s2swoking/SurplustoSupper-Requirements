# Surplus to Supper Requirements

Welcome to the public requirements Wiki for the Surplus to Supper operations platform.

The platform is being designed for Woking, Kingston, and future S2S branches. It covers receiving donated stock, inventory, recipient charities, allocation, loading, collection or delivery, returns, alerts, and reporting.
