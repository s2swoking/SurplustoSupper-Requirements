# Surplus to Supper Requirements

Welcome to the public requirements Wiki for the Surplus to Supper operations platform.

The platform is being designed for Woking, Kingston, and future S2S branches. It covers donor deliveries and Intake, inventory, recipient charities, Redistribution planning, loading, collection or delivery, returns, alerts, and reporting.
